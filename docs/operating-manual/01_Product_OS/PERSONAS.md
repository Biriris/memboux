# Personas

## Couple or organizer

Creates an event directly or receives ownership from a professional. Needs
simple setup, transparent package limits, durable control and reliable export.

## Guest

Views and contributes through a link or QR code with minimal friction. Does not
pay Memboux for ordinary participation.

## Independent professional

A planner, photographer or other approved provider serving a small number of
events. May prefer an event credit or a low-capacity subscription.

## Studio or agency

Manages multiple concurrent weddings and staff. Needs organization billing,
assignment, capacity, branding and auditable handoff.

## Venue or enterprise partner

Needs larger capacity, team controls, predictable support and potentially
contracted branding or integrations.

## Memboux workforce

Platform owners, administrators, operations, support, finance, moderation and
analyst staff. These roles are never reused as customer or professional roles.
