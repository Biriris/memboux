# Business Rules

## Baseline rules

- Events and media are private by default unless an explicit guest-access policy allows access.
- Permissions are server-enforced and event-scoped.
- Guests should encounter the least possible friction and must not pay to contribute memories.
- AI may assist users but must not weaken privacy, authorization, or human accountability.
- Data integrity, ownership continuity, and recoverable lifecycle transitions take priority over conversion shortcuts.
- Private photos, videos, identities, and guest behavior are not advertising inventory.

## Approved hybrid commercial direction

- Self-service wedding creation remains available.
- Guests do not pay for ordinary event participation.
- A useful free experience drives adoption without using private data for ads.
- Direct premium weddings use a one-time event entitlement.
- Professionals use subscriptions or event credits and may price their own
  client service freely.
- Memboux takes a marketplace commission only from a future transaction that it
  originates and facilitates.
- Event ownership, billing authority, professional organization membership and
  guest access are separate concerns.
- A failed or canceled professional subscription never removes couple ownership
  or deletes client memories.
- Consumer subscription renewal is used only for a separately defined recurring
  service, not disguised as a one-off event purchase.

The complete direction is in the
[Hybrid commercial model](HYBRID_COMMERCIAL_MODEL.md). It is not current
repository behavior. Until implemented, use the
[Repository Audit](../../repository-audit/EVENT_LIFECYCLE.md) for current event
