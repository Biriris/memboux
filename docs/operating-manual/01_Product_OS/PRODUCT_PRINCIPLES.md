# Product Principles

1. **Recover the missing perspective.** Help participants see the photographs
   and videos that would otherwise remain on separate phones.
2. **Guests participate freely.** Viewing and contribution should require the
   least safe amount of friction and no guest payment.
3. **People own their memories.** Event ownership and reasonable export must not
   depend on a planner's subscription or employment relationship.
4. **Charge for durable value.** Sell better experience, capacity, service and
   presentation through transparent packages, not artificial anxiety.
5. **Keep roles narrow.** Platform administration, billing, professional work
   and event ownership are separate permission boundaries.
6. **Privacy is part of the product.** Private media and participant behavior are
   not advertising inventory.
7. **Make commercial terms predictable.** Show limits, retention, price,
   renewal and cancellation before purchase or upload.
8. **Measure outcomes.** Validate activation, contribution, conversion,
   retention, trust and unit economics before scaling a package.
9. **Design for recovery.** Payment, invitation and lifecycle failures must not
   strand an event or remove its last valid owner.
