# Product Vision

Memboux is the operating system for shared event memories: one trusted place
where the photographs and videos captured across many phones become accessible
to the people whose lives they document.

The product serves three connected groups:

- guests, who contribute and discover memories without payment friction;
- couples and organizers, who own their event experience and data; and
- professionals, who deliver a premium client experience repeatedly.

Success is measured first by activated events, meaningful guest contribution,
successful memory export and returning organizers. Revenue grows through
transparent premium event purchases and professional relationships without
turning private memories into advertising inventory.
